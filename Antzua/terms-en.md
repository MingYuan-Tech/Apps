## 1. Service Description

Antzua is an iOS route planning and navigation tool designed for sales representatives, delivery drivers, and travel planners. Key features include:

- **Route Planning**: Given a starting point, multiple waypoints, and a destination, the smart algorithm automatically orders waypoints to plan the shortest, most efficient route
- **Address Management**: Add, edit, delete, and search addresses with group categories and business hours recording
- **Place Search**: Google Places API provides address search and autocomplete to quickly find destinations
- **Camera Scan Recognition**: Use the camera to scan and recognize addresses, names, phone numbers, and quickly create address entries
- **Turn-by-Turn Navigation**: 2D/3D route preview and turn-by-turn navigation with voice guidance, turn prompts, arrival confirmation, and automatic re-routing upon route deviation
- **Voice Navigation**: Multi-language voice announcements for turn instructions and arrival alerts, with customizable volume, speed, and voice
- **External Navigation App Launch**: Send destinations to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze for navigation
- **iCloud Sync**: Address and trip data automatically syncs via Apple CloudKit across devices with the same Apple ID
- **Multi-Language**: Supports Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean interface languages
- **Subscription Service**: Full features available through subscription plans, including a free trial period
